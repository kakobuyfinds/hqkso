Sizing, QC and shipping

Before you buy or ship

  1. Open the product listing, find the original seller URL when available, and check whether CSSBuy accepts it before paying.
  2. For shoes, compare foot length with the seller’s chart and confirm what the chart measures. For tops, compare flat chest width and length with a garment you already wear.
  3. When warehouse QC photos are available, match the size label and color to your order. Request a close-up or a measurement if a photo leaves a detail unclear.
  4. Get a shipping quote for your destination. If the route uses volumetric weight, compare the quote with and without extra packaging before approving the parcel.

CSSBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

It is a catalog or shortlist of product links used to compare items before buying through a shopping agent. This independent guide uses the HipobuyFind catalog as its product source.

Can I order every listing through CSSBuy?

Compatibility depends on the original seller link and CSSBuy’s acceptance of the item. Check the link in CSSBuy and confirm the exact variant before placing an order.

Where do I find QC photos?

Look for warehouse photos in your agent order after the item arrives, if the order includes that service. Use the seller gallery to compare the intended style and color before ordering.

Does the catalog price include delivery?

The cards show reference item prices. Check domestic delivery, international shipping, service fees and any applicable taxes separately when comparing your total.
